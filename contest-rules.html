Official Rules

Dead Culture

Poetry & Short Story Contest — last updated 22 September 2026

1 Eligibility

  • All contestants must be over the age of 18.
  • Entry is open to anyone worldwide unless otherwise stated.

2 Entry requirements

  • Only submissions in English will be accepted.
  • No AI-generated submissions. Work will be checked, and any AI-generated entries will be disqualified.
  • Submissions must be your own original, previously unpublished work.
  • Entries must not exceed 500 words.
  • One entry per person per fee paid.

3 Fees & payment

  • The entry fee is £5, payable via PayPal before submitting.
  • Entry fees are non-refundable, including for disqualified entries.

4 Deadline

Entries must be received by 31 October 2026. Late entries will not be considered.

5 Judging

  • Entries are judged on originality, craft, and impact.
  • The judges' decision is final, and no correspondence will be entered into regarding results.
  • Winners will be notified by email within 14 days of the deadline.

6 Prizes

First prize: £100 and publication on our Instagram, @Dead_Culture_Press. Details of any further prizes will be announced alongside the winner.

7 Rights & copyright

You retain full copyright of your work. By entering, you grant the organiser a one-time, non-exclusive right to publish your work on Instagram (with credit to you) if it is shortlisted or wins.

8 Disqualification

The organiser reserves the right to disqualify any entry that breaches these rules, without refund of the entry fee.

9 Your data

Your name, email, and Instagram handle are used only to administer this contest and contact winners. They will not be shared with third parties or used for any other purpose.

10 Contact

Questions about these rules or your entry? Message us on Instagram @Dead_Culture_Press.
